Source set: only the five supplied Q2FY27 earnings-call transcript PDFs in `co-working companies/`. Each PDF includes the management presentation, financial discussion and the full Q&A through the final page. PDF references below use physical PDF page numbers, including front-matter pages. Amounts are in ₹ crore unless specified.

## AWFIS Space Solutions Ltd

- **Reported vs normalized measures:** Reported Q2 revenue ₹439 Cr (+20% YoY), reported EBITDA ₹174 Cr (+31%), and reported margin 39.6%. Management separately presented normalized revenue ₹451 Cr (+26%) and cash EBITDA ₹49 Cr (+45%) at 10.8% margin. H1 reported revenue ₹864 Cr (+23%) and EBITDA ₹336 Cr (+30%); normalized revenue ₹888 Cr (+30%) and cash EBITDA ₹93 Cr (+40%) at 10.5%. Normalization adjusts Ind AS 116 finance-lease recognition; cash EBITDA also deducts actual cash lease rentals and excludes certain non-cash items. **Use the normalized/cash measures as such; do not compare them interchangeably with reported EBITDA.** (PDF pp. 5, 10)
- **Q2/H1 business figures:** Co-working revenue ₹376 Cr in Q2 (+31%) and ₹740 Cr in H1 (+34%). Transform revenue ₹75 Cr (+8%) in Q2 and ₹149 Cr (+16%) in H1. Transform is project-milestone driven; its ₹220 Cr order book/advance pipeline is presented as H2 visibility. (PDF p. 5; third-party share and pipeline also p. 12)
- **Network/occupancy:** As at Sep. 30, 244 operating centers plus 10 under fit-out (254 total supply); >164,000 operational seats, ~174,000 including fit-out and ~188,000 including signed LOIs. Q2 gross additions ~7,000 seats and exits ~2,200; H1 additions ~12,000 seats. Occupancy: 76.2% blended, 84.1% at centers operating >12 months. Gold/Elite: 39 centers and >33,000 seats. (PDF pp. 8-9; premium detail and pricing pp. 6, 13)
- **Guidance:** FY27 additions 22,000-25,000 gross seats; expected total operational seat portfolio >174,000 at March end after ~5,500 expected closures, ~4,000 already closed in H1. FY27 co-working revenue growth 23-25%, Transform ~20% (management later said ~₹300+ Cr), total revenue >₹1,800 Cr, cash EBITDA ₹190-200 Cr. H2 is expected to be stronger. Q&A: Q4 cash EBITDA margin ~11.5%, March exit ~12%; management agreed 12% is the FY28 starting floor. (PDF pp. 7, 11-12)
- **Quote:** Amit Ramani: “On profitability, we expect full-year cash EBITDA of INR 190 crores to INR 200 crores, with the second half stronger than the first as occupancy at recently launched centers continues to build and the premium cohort matures.” (PDF p. 7). This is the complete sentence, with speaker Amit Ramani.
- **Risks / caveats from Q&A:** About 3,000 seats were vacated by a large enterprise client in May; by the call, 57-58% was sold/committed, with revenue expected on 55-60% by December; balance remained in pipeline. New centers and premium supply are ramping, though management expects occupancy to build. Transform quarterly revenue varies with project milestones. (PDF pp. 5, 14)

## EFC (I) Ltd

- **Q2/H1 reported figures:** Q2 consolidated revenue from operations was ₹332.02 Cr (+30% YoY), EBITDA ₹133.77 Cr (+21%), and PAT ₹77.26 Cr (+36%); the official filing gives Q2 EBITDA margin as 40%. H1 revenue from operations was ₹614.90 Cr versus ₹474.21 Cr (+30%), EBITDA ₹256.73 Cr (+20%), PAT ₹148.16 Cr (+43%), and EBITDA margin 42%. The transcript’s verbal H1 revenue figure of “approximately 650 crores” is arithmetically inconsistent with its claimed 30% growth and with the official statement; the draft uses the filed figure. The NSE investor presentation consolidated P&L is on PDF p. 37 (slide 36): [official NSE filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/EFCILTD_03112026022250_Intimation.pdf). Transcript discussion: PDF pp. 9-10.
- **Segment figures:** Q2 Leasing ~₹168 Cr (+30%); Design & Build ~₹129 Cr (+16%); Furniture ~₹35 Cr (+149%). The official presentation reports H1 Leasing ₹321 Cr (+28%), D&B ₹230 Cr (+17%), Furniture ₹63 Cr (+137%) (slide 5 / PDF p. 6); these rounded segment figures total ₹614 Cr, consistent with filed H1 revenue ₹614.90 Cr. The transcript's ₹322 Cr leasing figure and implied ~₹615 Cr segment sum differ slightly from the presentation. Growth is against a smaller Furniture base; D&B timing depends on project execution and milestones. (Transcript PDF pp. 10-11; official NSE presentation p. 6)
- **Guidance from Q&A:** Management restated D&B FY27 growth target of 50-60%, citing an order book >₹300 Cr. The Q&A also mentions 20-22% consolidated and several segment-level EBITDA margins, but the transcript does not define a normalization or alternative basis. The official filing gives reported EBITDA margins of 40% for Q2 and 42% for H1; the Q&A margin guidance therefore cannot be reconciled and is omitted from the draft JSON. (Transcript PDF pp. 12-14; official NSE presentation PDF p. 37)
- **Quote:** Nikhil Bhuta: “Absolutely, we are on target to achieve the annual target that we have set for our Design & Build vertical.” (PDF p. 14). This is a complete sentence verbatim; the main JSON entry omits the ambiguous margin statements.
- **Important strategic developments:** Proposed demerger/restructuring of asset-light managed office operations and owned-property business remains subject to approvals, including NCLT. Ultrafresh acquisition was described as a ₹54 Cr share-swap acquisition; management cited modular furniture, North India manufacturing and 160+ touchpoints as strategic rationale. (PDF pp. 6-8, 16)
- **Risks / caveats:** D&B H1 growth of ~17% was below the FY27 50-60% target, with management relying on project execution in H2 and an order book >₹300 Cr. Q2 consolidated EBITDA growth trailed revenue growth; management attributed quarterly margin variation to project timing and increased material/service costs, while maintaining annual targets. Working capital, timely collections, project execution and inventory/capacity utilization are explicit priorities. (PDF pp. 10-14)

## Indiqube Spaces Ltd

- **Q2/H1 figures:** Q2 revenue ~₹475 Cr (+34% YoY), EBITDA ₹105 Cr (+40%) on I-GAAP basis, PAT ₹57 Cr (+63%). H1 revenue ₹904 Cr (+35%), PAT ₹92 Cr (+66%). The call does not provide Q2/H1 EBITDA total in its verbal financial summary beyond the Q2 I-GAAP amount; avoid inferring H1 EBITDA. H1 VAS revenue ₹154 Cr (+74%), ~17% of operating revenue vs 13% last year. (PDF pp. 4-5)
- **Operating / platform figures:** 10.86 mn sq ft, 138 centers, ~241,000 seats and 884 clients. 3.8 mn sq ft/~85,000 seats headroom, of which 2.67 mn sq ft has LOIs for anticipated operation over 12-18 months. Steady-state occupancy 91% vs 89% a year earlier. (PDF pp. 3-5)
- **Guidance:** FY27 rent-paying addition close to 2 mn sq ft (~44,000 seats). FY27 full-year EBITDA margin 19-21%, with management indicating ₹380-400 Cr EBITDA. Similar area-addition scale was anticipated for FY28, but this is directional management commentary, not a precise formal guide. For solar, a 30 MW expansion (~₹100 Cr capex) was underway and a similar investment was planned for next year. (PDF pp. 7-8)
- **Solar and adjacencies:** ~30 MW operational; another ~30 MW expected within a year. H1 solar revenue ~₹15 Cr on ~₹46 Cr investment. Management stated overall VAS margin improved from 15% to 20%, but said VAS could range around 18-20% because growth is a priority; it is not an unconditional margin floor. (PDF pp. 5-8)
- **Quote:** Rishi Das: “We will be range bound. Our EBITDA will be between, I would say, 19% to 21% in that range is the full year guidance that we see.” (PDF p. 7)
- **Risks / caveats:** Management estimated ~10% annual attrition and said ~90% of customers renew; among renewals, some have reduced escalations or discounts. SaiEdge is an early pilot co-development/property investment; the Bangalore land-stage project is about three years from readiness, and management said further rollout depends on the pilot. FY28 area figure is similar-scale directional commentary. Working capital and management bandwidth arose in Q&A; do not present adjacencies as risk-free or immediately revenue-accretive. (PDF pp. 8-12, 14-17)

## Smartworks Coworking Spaces Ltd

- **Q2/H1 normalized and reported figures:** Q2 revenue from operations ₹591 Cr (+39% YoY; +8% QoQ), normalized EBITDA ₹117 Cr (+68%) at 19.8% margin, normalized PAT ₹43 Cr (+136%), and reported PAT ₹26 Cr. H1 revenue growth 41%; normalized EBITDA margin 19.7%. Normalized operating cash flow was ₹202 Cr for H1; H1 free cash flow was negative ~₹104 Cr. (PDF pp. 5-6)
- **Occupancy/capacity:** Platform 17.5 mn sq ft / 71 centers, including 10.9 mn sq ft / 58 operational. Q2 overall occupancy ~81%, 86% committed; mature centers (9.7 mn sq ft) 88% occupied / 91% committed; new campuses 48% committed. H2 planned openings total ~45,000 seats, mainly Eastside and East Bridge; new supply may temporarily push blended occupancy below 80%. (PDF pp. 4, 6-8)
- **Guidance:** Revenue growth raised to 33-35% from 28-30%; normalized EBITDA margin 19-20% (trending to upper end); operational footprint ~13 mn sq ft by Mar. 2027 (more than 12.5 mn); FY27 capex ₹550-600 Cr, with ₹307 Cr deployed H1; annual area additions 2.5-3 mn sq ft. (PDF pp. 4-6)
- **Quote:** Neetish Sarda: “On the back of this, we are raising our FY '27 revenue growth guidance to 33% to 35% from 28% to 30%.” (PDF p. 4)
- **Risks / caveats:** Negative H1 free cash flow reflects capex and security deposits preceding center revenue; management expects below-1x reported cash conversion while booking new supply. Net debt was ~₹83 Cr. Management explicitly warned temporary occupancy dip with mega-campus openings. Some transcript figures have editorial corrections: growth guidance corrected from a spoken 31% to 33-35%; borrowing cost corrected to <9% (8.5% was noted as an error); other footnotes also correct Eastside/East Bridge timing/supply references. Use corrected transcript text/footnotes, not superseded spoken values. (PDF pp. 5-8; corrections pp. 4-7)

## Uncertainties / omissions to retain in the draft

- EFC's prepared remarks say H1 revenue was approximately ₹650 Cr while also stating ~30% growth; those figures do not reconcile. The official NSE filing confirms ₹614.90 Cr vs ₹474.21 Cr (+30%), and H1 segment revenues sum to approximately ₹614 Cr. The transcript's consolidated EBITDA-margin guidance of 20-22% and segment margins also conflict with the filed 40% Q2 / 42% H1 EBITDA margins; their basis is unspecified, so the JSON omits them. Segment growth and D&B order book/target should not be interpreted as a smooth quarterly run rate.
- IndiQube's FY28 area addition was directional (similar to FY27), and the SaiEdge pilot remains formative. No annualized Q2 EBITDA should be extrapolated from the transcript.
- Smartworks's transcript contains explicit correction footnotes; the values in the draft use the corrected figures. The overall occupancy dip is management's expected H2 scenario as new centers open, not a current realized decline.
- WeWork India provides I-GAAP-equivalent and statutory Ind AS PAT that differ substantially. Keep labels explicit in any downstream display or comparison.

## Assessment rule

“Met” or “missed” requires a target whose deadline has passed and an actual figure with the same definition. Most Q1 guidance is for the full year, ending March 2027. For those items, state the Q2/H1 run rate, guidance change, and remaining work; do not put an FY27 verdict in the site's `guidanceTracking` table yet.

## Publishing guardrails

- Any `guidanceTracking` row for Q2 should compare a **Q2 due** target with a Q2 actual on the same basis. Annual aims belong in guidance/outlook and highlights until their deadline.
- State H1 progress in plain language, including slower pacing and explicit guidance changes. “On pace” is an interim judgment, not a “met” status.
- Keep reported revenue, normalized revenue, rental area, signed area, operational area, and capacity separate. Their definitions differ across companies and sometimes within a call.
